## Ahoy, and welcome

Monotonic Labs is a privacy-focused machine learning lab building small, capable AI systems that run at the edge, on the hardware individuals and small businesses already own. We believe useful intelligence should not require handing your data to someone else's server, and we design every system around that conviction from the very first line.

## What we build

- **Small models, well tuned** - compact systems shaped for laptops, phones, and modest on-premise hardware rather than sprawling data centers.
- **Edge-first applications** - tools that do their work locally, so they stay fast, dependable, and available even when the network is not.
- **Practical AI for SMBs** - focused solutions for small and medium businesses that want the benefits of machine learning without the overhead of a cloud contract.

## What we believe

- **Privacy is the default, not a feature** - your data stays with you, and we design so it never needs to leave.
- **Smaller is often better** - an elegant, efficient model you can run and understand beats a grandiose one you can only rent.
- **Progress only moves one way** - like our namesake, we aim to improve steadily and never regress, release after release.
